AGENT, NOT JUST ASSISTANT
### Move from useful responses to accountable business work.
An enterprise agent is designed around a defined role, approved context, permitted actions, operational controls, and an intended business outcome.
Comparison table, GENERIC ASSISTANT vs ALGOMOTIVE ENTERPRISE AGENT:
01 Purpose - Responds to user questions or requests. | Operates toward a defined business responsibility and measurable outcome.
02 Context - Primarily uses the current conversation. | Uses approved enterprise knowledge, records, workflow state, and organizational boundaries.
03 Action - Usually produces information or content. | Can coordinate governed workflows and invoke approved tools or system actions.
04 Control - Relies mainly on user judgment after the response. | Applies policies, permissions, risk thresholds, approvals, and execution limits before action.
05 Accountability - Conversation history may provide limited traceability. | Can preserve the event, context, versions, decisions, approvals, actions, and results.
06 Measurement - Success is often based on response usefulness. | Success is evaluated through workflow health, control coverage, and business outcomes.

- Policy enforcement

- Approved tools
- Protected credentials
- Workflow execution
- Validated responses
#### Measure

- Audit evidence

ENTERPRISE AGENT ANATOMY

#### Approved Context

#### Instructions and Policies
Business rules, process instructions, risk thresholds, decision boundaries, and governance requirements guide the agent.
#### Scoped Tools
Tool access is limited to the approved functions, systems, actions, destinations, and permission levels required by the workflow.
#### Memory Boundaries
Where memory is required, its scope, purpose, retention, access, validation, and isolation are defined for the implementation.
#### Structured Outputs
Decisions, recommendations, tool requests, exceptions, and downstream actions use validated structures rather than unrestricted text.
#### Human Authority
Sensitive, exceptional, ambiguous, or high-impact decisions can be routed to an authorized person with supporting evidence.
#### Observable Execution
